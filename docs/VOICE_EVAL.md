## What this does not cover

- Speech: recognition errors, accents, background noise and interruptions are not simulated; the harness
  feeds text. Those need real calls (see docs/VOICE.md).
- Without the model, descriptions that name no option ("red itchy patches") are asked again and then
  handed to a care coordinator; that is the expected deterministic failure above, not a safety issue.
